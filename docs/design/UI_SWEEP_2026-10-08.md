# UI quality sweep, 8 October 2026

Every screen of the screenshot manifest (`tools/screenshots/screens.cjs`) was captured on the seed profile at
1440 x 900, before and after the sweep, with a presentation check on each page. The complained screens and the
group profile picker were also captured at 1920 x 1080. The rules below are now part of the shared components, so
new screens follow them without screen-specific styles.

## Rules applied to every screen

- **Tiles.** Home pages' tiles use one anatomy and height; labels in Title Case with acronyms kept; linked tiles
  are not underlined; long labels take at most two lines and the counts of a row line up.
- **Titles.** Card titles and tile labels are written in Title Case (the callout names stay as written).
- **Page header.** The title block takes the room left and wraps its description; the page actions stay on the
  right instead of dropping under the title.
- **Tabs.** A tab strip never wraps onto a second row; a long strip scrolls sideways.
- **Toolbars.** Toolbar controls (search, buttons, toggles, check boxes) share one height (40 px) and line up.
- **Tables.** Every column has a header; the actions column reads "Actions". Long header labels wrap between words
  instead of widening the table past its card. A code column keeps its width (the name under the code wraps); a
  two-line text cell is never squeezed to one word per line; codes never break.
- **Values.** Missing amounts show the muted dash; amounts and dates inside texts from the server are formatted
  (PHP 2,500.00, 01-Dec-2027); counts read in the right number everywhere ("1 account", "Showing 1 to 1 of 1
  result"), no more "account(s)"; two values in one cell are a two-line cell, never "a · b".
- **Workflow actions.** The action buttons of a record wrap inside the workflow header; one filled primary action
  per area, the other routes outlined, exclusion and closing outlined red.
- **Names, not codes.** Segment and sales unit of the PR Worklist by name.

## Round 2 (9 October 2026)

- **Wide lists.** Every list of eight columns or more, and every list with a column hidden by default, has a
  column chooser above it (show, hide and order the columns; saved per user in the browser, Reset to Default), a
  Compact / Comfortable row toggle and a sticky first column (the reference, with the selection box). No business
  column is removed: the defaults of the 30 wide lists show what fits 1440 px and the other columns stay one click
  away in the chooser and in the Excel export (`DataTable` `defaultHidden`, `TableTools`, `useColumnChoice`).
- **My Work.** Names, types and descriptions are shown whole (wrapped in their column, the full text in the
  tooltip); the Amount column shows only when a work item of the list has an amount; "My Queues".
- **Finance dashboard.** The ledger and workload tiles use the KPI tile: journals in progress and pending approvals
  as labelled breakdown lines, never "0 pending · 0 draft".
- **Remittance records.** The line under the title is one sentence; the disbursement and the hold dates are
  two-line values; remarks are a key fact.
- **One scroll area.** The access matrix is capped to the room left in the window; the report batch dialog
  scrolls as one.
- **Ages.** An item dated in the future reads "Not yet due (in 389 days)", never a negative age.
- **Server texts.** Insurer names are readable by the screens that show them (submitted policies, renewal, claims
  handling, collections, employee benefits, contact centre); report and job descriptions name the setting in
  business words instead of its parameter code.
- **Menu.** The group holding Claims Handling is named "Claims" now that the insurer modules are removed.

## Round 3 (9 October 2026)

- **One dialog standard.** `Modal` sizes (sm 480, md 640, lg 920 px; by content when not given), Title Case
  titles naming the object ("Resolve Alert ALR-000012"), a key-facts block and a helper line, Cancel (secondary)
  to the left of the primary action on every dialog (added where a footer had no way back; "Go Back" next to a
  Cancel action), Escape closes the top dialog only and never while the action runs, Enter in a field confirms,
  the focus moves in, stays in and returns. `ConfirmDialog` and `ActionDialog` take the key facts, a comment or
  reason with its counter and least / most characters, the problem under the field, and the danger style with
  "This cannot be undone." Applies to all 301 dialogs of the shared `Modal`; nine record dialogs now name their record.
- **Alerts.** Rules by their business name everywhere (list, filter, dialog, toast, exception report); the alert
  reference ALR-nnnnnn; acknowledge / resolve in the row action menu and a dialog with the rule, severity, raised
  on, record (linked where it has a page), status and what happened. Server-built alert and notification texts
  write dates as dd-MMM-yyyy, amounts with separators, codes in words and counted nouns in the singular or plural.
- **Searchable lists.** `Combobox` (search by name, code or hint; keyboard; clear) for High-risk Clients (risk
  category, marketing unit, client type), the alert rule, currencies (8 forms), cost centres (6), users of the
  audit and archive logs, sales units, and every list of values, insurer list and Report Centre parameter with
  twelve values or more. The High-risk list shows names instead of codes and user ids.
- **Empty lists keep their headers.** The table renders its headers with the empty message (and the next action
  where allowed, "Add Incentive Criterion") in 14 lists, tabs and edit grids that used to show a bare message.
- **Loading and toasts.** 77 record, tab and panel loads show a skeleton panel instead of a lone spinner; toasts
  start with a capital, stay 5 s (warnings 8 s, errors 10 s) and close with their button.
- **Report Centre.** Every report has one to three standard variants (configuration, `V2610`), listed under
  "Standard" before "My Variants" and "Shared by Others"; the report opens with the user's default variant or the
  first standard one; "Choose a variant"; Save Variant names the variant, who sees it and whether it is the
  default; standard variants cannot be deleted. List parameters (users, insurers, sales units, product lines,
  products, segments, cost centres, bank accounts, group profiles, modules, risk categories, alert rules,
  schedules, SOA uploads) are searchable choices with business labels; "only / all except" lists are chips. Run
  Report is the primary action with "Download as" next to it; Print after a run; the result table always shows
  its headers; the user's recent runs below. Report descriptions drop requirement references (34 reports).
  All 293 reports were run with their first standard variant on the seed data: 226 return rows (50 of them only
  since this round: period, grouping or schedule chosen to fit the report), 64 return none because the seed holds
  no such records, 3 wait for the user's choice of an SOA upload or bank account. 168 list parameters of 89
  reports are searchable choices, 106 "only / all except" lists are chips.

## Findings left open

| Finding | Why open |
|---|---|
| Lists of Values, Document Templates and Coverages show their codes next to the names | Administration screens where the code is the key the administrator maintains |
| 63 reports return no rows with their standard variant on the seed data | The seed holds no records of those processes (tax books of other forms, minimum balances, cancellations, migration trial runs...); the reports run without error |
| Three reports need the user's choice before they run (SOA reconciliation and upload log, bank reconciliation statement) | The SOA upload or bank account is chosen from the list; the seed holds no SOA upload |
| Configuration Promotion carries `nbr_report_variant` as a user dataset only | The catalogue of the configpromo branch (`NBR_REPORT_VARIANT`, `users: true`) should carry the standard rows (`standard = true`) as configuration and the users' rows as user data |
| The BRD-11 sign-off set describes the group profiles as check boxes by module | The set's screen texts and images to refresh with the next BRD-11 pack run (the recipe already selects through the new picker) |
