# BDOI target-state group profiles

The group profiles of the BDOI User Access Matrix (target state, August 2026) as BIBS group profiles,
for the DEV, SIT and UAT environments. Each role column of the matrix is one group profile `BDOI_*`;
the profile holds the permissions its ticked capabilities map to, so it opens the menus, screens and
workflow stages (maker, checker, approver) the matrix gives the role. The profiles are created and
kept in their target state by the seed script `V2900__seed_bdoi_target_state_profiles.sql` (seed
profile only, never in production). Users are given a profile through the membership event of the
identity simulator, as UIDM-ISC does in production (`tools/dev/load_personnel.py`).

This document holds no personnel data.

## Sources and rules

- **Matrix.** Sheet `CLEAN` is the reference. For a capability that `CLEAN` leaves blank for every
  role (for example the Accounting, Refund, ACSL and Disbursement rows) and for the role columns that
  `CLEAN` does not have (Collections Team Head / Team Lead / Officer, Technical Services and
  Reinsurance Unit Head, Analytics, Risk Management, Compliance Checker / Officer, Trade Processor),
  the sheet `Updated User Access Matrix` is used. The capabilities only `CLEAN` lists are added. The
  sheet `Additional for Renewal` lists functions without role columns; their permissions are below.
  The Business System Administrator has no target-state column: it comes from the current-state sheet
  and is the checker of the business configuration that the MBS profile maintains.
- **Ticks.** A tick, and a tick with a segment qualifier ("Retail", "Corporate", "for Renewal
  Handlers only"), grants the capability. The segment is not part of a group profile: it is the
  user's data access (business units), set per user on the User Access screens. "viewing only" and
  "Read Only" grant the read permissions of the capability only. "Corporate X" does not grant it.
- **Every profile** holds `DASHBOARD_VIEW` and `WORK_VIEW` (sign-in, home page and My Work).
- **System behaviour** (reference numbers, notifications, logging, password-protected documents,
  automated accounting entries) needs no permission: it applies to everyone using the function.
- **Workflow stages.** The maker, checker and approver stages of the workflows are permissions
  (`*_REQUEST` / `*_PROCESS` makers, `*_APPROVE` / `*_AUTHORIZE` checkers). The matrix states most of
  them ("Approve ...", "Posting of transactions", "Batch posting"). Where the matrix names the work
  but no column holds its checker or its assignment, the head of the team holds it (table "Workflow
  stages added"). The system refuses the approval of one's own work whatever the profile holds.
- **Menus** are the sections of the web client whose screens one of the profile's permissions
  opens (navigation `modules.ts`; a section opens when any of its screens opens).

## Matrix ticks not granted

The screening rows of the updated matrix give the Marketing roles (the investigators of BRD-10) and
MBS some decisions that BIBS keeps with Compliance: the watchlist and configuration maker and checker,
the compliance review of escalations and STRs, the AML Committee and the STR extract. Separation of
duties requires them to stay with the Compliance profiles; the investigation itself (case data,
disposition, risk profile, documents, case approval) is granted. To be confirmed by BDOI Compliance.
