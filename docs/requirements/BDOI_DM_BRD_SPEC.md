## 2. Business summary

**What BRD-13 asks for.** BDOI replaces its legacy platforms, named in the BRD as "QPS and EBIX" (p.3), with one core system. The other BRDs also name **ISYS** (claims and ACSL reports, Marketing Diary, ARF) and **Excel** masterlists (Submitted Policies). The BRD does not ask for a full history conversion. It asks for a **selective, continuity-focused** migration (p.3):

| Treatment | What it means | BRD |
|---|---|---|
| **Migrate** | Clean, high-value master and reference data needed on Day 1: Client Master, LOV, insurer / product / package / risk codes, MIS | p.3, p.5; BRID 2.1, 3.1 |
| **Carry forward (open items)** | Only the open operational financial positions needed to continue operations: unapplied premium payments (UPP), outstanding receivables, remittance positions | p.3; BRID 5.1-8.1 |
| **Archive** (read-only legacy or archival access) | Historical transactions and documents stay in the legacy systems in read-only mode or in an archive, with logged access | p.3, p.5; BRID 11.1 |
| **Excluded** | Anything that fails the decision gates | BRID 1.1a |
| **Conditional: in-force policy header** | Minimal header of in-force policies (status, key dates, insurer, line of business, client link) when operationally required | BRID 4.1 |

The four classes are the outcome of a **decision gate per data object** (BRID 1.1a), and every migrated object must be **reconcilable from source to target** (BRID 1.1b).

**The legacy invoice.** An invoice booked in EBIX / QPS before go-live that is still open at cutover. It is not re-booked in BIBS. It is carried forward with its open balances and then **processed in BIBS** for the rest of its life: payments (OTC, autopay), UPP automatch rerun across legacy and new invoices, UPP dispositions, DPPR and PR2307 batch reversals, remittance, endorsements, and production reconciliation (BRID 5-10). The BRD asks for **legacy sub-ledgers** next to the new ones: Premium Receivable (legacy), UPP (legacy), Commission Receivable (legacy) and DTIP (legacy) (BRID 5.2, 6.1, 6.3, 6.4, 7.1, 7.2, 8.1).

**Renewal-driven transition.** The cutover is aligned to the renewal expiry month (RMEL) (BRID 12.1). In-force legacy policies run off; at renewal they are recreated as clean policy and account records in BIBS (p.5). The legacy systems stay read-only for inquiry and audit during run-off and are then decommissioned against agreed criteria (BRID 12.1).

**Personas.** Data Migration Lead (1.1a, 1.1b), Marketing User (2.1), Data Steward (3.1), Operations User (4.1, 9.1-9.3), Cashiering User (5.1-5.4, 6.1-6.4), Finance Approver (5.5), Accounting User (7.1, 7.2), Remittance User (8.1), Prod Recon Analyst (10.1), Audit / Compliance User (11.1), Program Manager (12.1). The Data Migration Lead, Data Steward and Finance Approver are new personas in BIBS; "top management" (5.5) is not defined (DMQ16).

**What the BRD does not say** (all raised as questions in section 10):
- the list of data objects, their source systems, owners, volumes and historic depth (DMQ01, DMQ03, DMQ28);
- the "agreed keys" for client dedupe (DMQ04);
- the condition of the in-force header migration and the "coexistence decision" for endorsements (DMQ09, DMQ22);
- GL opening balances, although carried-forward receivables need them (DMQ18);
- the cutover date, window, freeze, rehearsals, fallback and decommissioning criteria (DMQ25-DMQ27, DMQ31, DMQ32);
- the NFRs: every usage table points to a "consolidated NFR" document that is not in the pack (DMQ29);
- claims, Employee Benefits programmes, submitted-policy masterlists, payees and users, which other BRDs expect to migrate (DMQ30).

### Big-ticket items

1. **Migration platform (BRID 1.1a, 1.1b, 3.1).** Nothing in BIBS governs a migration today. The bulk framework (`bulk/service/BulkService.java`) is built for business uploads: 5,000 rows per file (`BULK_MAX_ROWS`), one transaction per row, no control totals, no object register, no mapping versions, no reconciliation and no sign-off. A new `migration` module is needed: data object register with decision gates, extract intake with control totals and checksums, a staging area with a 5-day purge, versioned code maps, a data-quality rule catalogue, loaders that call the domain services, batches with run log, rerun and rollback, reconciliation per object (counts, amounts, hash totals, field-level source-to-target) and sign-off gates.
2. **Legacy invoice coexistence (BRID 5-10).** The Operations ledger (`ops_invoice`) receives invoices only from booking (`opsledger/service/InvoiceLedgerWriter.java` reads `BookingQueryService.byNo`; `FeedSource` is EVENT or REPLAY). A legacy invoice needs a ledger origin (LEGACY), a source system, its legacy number, opening positions by component (paid and remitted before cutover), and a **ledger context** that routes every posting of the invoice and of legacy UPP to the legacy control accounts. Seven built modules post against the ledger and each needs the legacy components: cashiering, remittance, commission, adjustment (through booking), prodrecon, collections and ACSL.
3. **Endorsements on legacy invoices (BRID 9.1-9.3).** Adjustment reads the account by ARN (`adjustment/service/PremiumDeltas.java` line 172, `AdjustmentDocuments.java` line 121) and booking posts endorsements only on a booked BIBS original (`booking/service/EndorsementPostingService.post`: `requireBooked(arn)`, `originalOf(arn, date)`). Endorsing a legacy invoice therefore needs the in-force policy header as a BIBS account (BRID 4.1) and a booking port that supplies the legacy original. BRID 9 and BRID 4.1 depend on each other (DMQ09, DMQ22).
4. **Client master with dedupe (BRID 2.1).** `crm/service/DuplicateCheckService.java` has hard keys (TIN, ID type and number, last and first name with birth date) and soft keys (e-mail, mobile, corporate name). The client bulk upload (`crm/service/ClientBulkHandler.java`) creates **prospects**, not clients, and every new client fires `ClientRegistered`, which triggers sanction screening one client at a time (`screening/matching/service/ScreeningTriggers.java`). Migration needs a registration path for active clients, survivorship rules for legacy duplicates, a legacy key cross-reference, and screening of the migrated base as one run after the load.
5. **Cutover, RMEL run-off and archive (BRID 11.1, 12.1).** A cutover plan with mock runs and a go / no-go gate, a go-live extraction of the January-May 2028 expiries from the migrated headers through the Renewal port (`renewal.service.port.LegacyPolicySource`, RENEWAL_DESIGN section 2.2), a provisional GL opening at the year-end boundary with controlled FY2027 true-ups (DMQ39), a run-off tracker, decommissioning criteria, and a read-only inquiry with access logging. The audit trail has no read action today (`audit/domain/AuditAction.java`), so access logging is new.

## 9. Observations on the BRD draft

- **Draft, unsigned.** Version 0.01 of 14-Apr-2026 (p.2); every signature and date on the approval pages is empty (p.15-16). The baseline may change when the BRD is walked through (p.16 note).
- **Template text left in.** The cover says "Business Requirements Document (BRD) Template" (p.1). The business-goal answers ("operational efficiency", "improved servicing reliability", "Risk Reduction and Data Quality Improvement (clean start)") are in the blue sample style, and the footer "Texts highlighted in blue are provided as samples only. Please remove them when drafting the actual BRD" is still on p.3. The template instructions of section V (p.6) and the usage-table guidance (p.13) are also still in the text.
- **Business goals.** "Compliance / Regulatory Requirement" and "Tech Obsolescence" are not ticked (p.3), although BRID 11.1 serves audit and regulatory inquiries and the programme replaces legacy platforms.
- **Duplicate ID.** BRID 1.1 is used for two requirements (p.7): decision gates and reconciliation. This baseline calls them 1.1a and 1.1b.
- **Incomplete acceptance criterion.** BRID 1.1a reads "Then it data objects been actioned upon ie. as Migrate / Carry-Forward (Open Items) / Archive / Excluded" (p.7). The rule that decides the class is missing (DMQ02).
- **Empty usage tables.** Volume and performance, availability and retention all point to a consolidated NFR document that is not named by version and not in the pack (p.13-14); the table rows are empty. There are no migration volumes, no cutover window and no data-quality thresholds (DMQ28, DMQ29).
- **Conditional capabilities with "Must have" rows.** Capability 4 is "conditional" and capability 9 is "subject to coexistence decision" (p.6), but BRID 4.1 and 9.1-9.3 are "Must have" and the condition and the decision are not stated. BRID 9 cannot be met without BRID 4.1 in BIBS (section 3 big-ticket 3; DMQ09, DMQ22).
- **No data object list and one-sided system list.** Only QPS and EBIX are named, "e.g." (p.3). ISYS and the Excel masterlists that other BRDs use are not mentioned, nor claims, EB programmes, submitted policies, payees or users (DMQ03, DMQ30).
- **GL not mentioned.** The BRD asks for legacy sub-ledgers and accounting entries on legacy invoices (BRID 5-8), but not for the GL opening balances those sub-ledgers must agree with (DMQ18).
- **Undefined terms.** UPP, DPPR, PR2307, DTIP, RMEL, OTC, autopay, MIS, LOV, Prod Recon, "Cash Receipts Books", "Cash Disbursements Books" and "top management" are used without definition. BIBS reads them as in the Operations, Collections and Accounting BRDs (FRS glossary); DPPR is read as "direct-payment premium receivable" (DMQ19).
- **Mixed transition model.** BRID 12.1 says "confirmed / booked / bound policies follow the agreed transition model", but the model is not described (DMQ26).
- **No cutover controls.** Mock runs, dress rehearsal, go / no-go, fallback and hypercare are not asked for; the design adds them because BRID 1.1b and 12.1 cannot be met without them.
- **Document title.** The page header reads "BDOI Core Modernization Data Migration"; the umbrella BRD is titled "BDOI CORE REPLACEMENT".
- **Early-renewal concept paper superseded.** The concept paper of 06-Sep-2026, signed by MANCOM, proposed a pre-cut-over renewal release on or before 15-Aug-2027 with early migration of the client master and all renewal-related reference data (p.1-2). BDOI decided on 26-Sep-2026 that everything goes live together in January 2028, so the concept paper is **superseded by the single January 2028 go-live** (register DCR-240). What still applies is listed in section 10.
