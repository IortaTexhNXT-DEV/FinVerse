## 2. Business summary

**What BRD-3 is about.** The main BRD covers the maintenance of **package products** (the addendum calls them "packaged products"): pre-arranged insurance programmes whose terms, rates and conditions BDOI negotiates with one or more insurers and then offers repeatedly through Marketing. Examples in the BDOI product master are the Fire (PAR) and Motor (MTR) packages of BRD-1 Appendix B and the "programs" of Annex I, such as `CAR11` "2021 BDOI CAR Program - Group A". Some packages are client-specific (BRPM.013 note). Today these packages are set up through e-mail, Excel comparative tables, manual sign-off and spreadsheets for expiry monitoring (p.12-13).

**The lifecycle that BRD-3 asks for (BRPM.008-017):**

| Step | Owner | What happens | BRD |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Package request | Marketing AO / TSU | Package Request Form with client information and the requested terms and conditions; validated before submission | BRPM.008 |
| 2. Marketing approval | Marketing TL, TH or UH | Approves the request before it reaches TSU | BRPM.008, BRPM.021 |
| 3. TSU review | TSU TL, then TSU TH | TSU TL reviews and recommends; TSU TH approves in the system | BRPM.009, BRPM.011 |
| 4. Negotiation | TSU (Officer / TL / TH) | Quotation Slip (QS) to insurers, approved by the TL or a co-officer and editable until ManCom sign-off; insurer terms keyed in; revision rounds; exception states | BRPM.010, BRPM.012, BRPM.013, PMADD04 |
| 5. Comparative table | System / TSU | One consolidated comparative table per package, with an audit master and client-tailored views; Marketing has view-only access and reviews the terms before any client presentation | BRPM.013, BRPM.014, PMADD03 |
| 6. ManCom sign-off | ManCom (Management Committee, p.49) | TSU submits the product requirements "computation and details that Marketing needs"; ManCom signs off in the system | BRPM.015 |
| 7. Package set-up | MBS (Marketing Business Services and System Support, p.8) | MBS receives only signed-off, complete requirements, returns incomplete ones to TSU, and sets up the package with its computation logic | BRPM.015 |
| 8. Post-set-up validation | TSU Head / Business Administrator | The configuration is not "ready" until it is validated; the validation is audited | PMADD06 |
| 9. Release and advisory | System, TSU TL / Officer, Operations | The package becomes available to Marketing, which is notified; an advisory with supporting documents (package slip, ManCom sign-off) goes to the relevant units | BRPM.015, BRPM.016 |
| 10. Monitoring and renewal | TSU Officer, MBS / Operations | Expiry or anniversary monitoring; a renewal request with the details pre-filled; approvals; renewal advisory; expired packages archived and kept searchable | BRPM.017, BRPM.006 |

**Cross-cutting rules:**
- New business always uses the latest approved rate scheme. Old packages are locked for new business but stay usable for renewals, and the scheme version is logged on each transaction (BRPM.007).
- There is one product hierarchy (LOB, then Subline / Type / Subtype, then Coverage / Peril), with insurer-specific coverage, deductible and warranty mappings (PMADD01, PMADD02).
- A role-to-action matrix (create / amend / approve / view-only) controls access (PMADD05).
- Incentive criteria, for example "CPC2", are maintained against the maintained products matrix, with effective dates and history (PMADD07, PMADD08).

**Overlap with BRD-1.** 13 of the 23 main-BRD rows restate BRD-1 rows almost word for word, applied here to packages instead of PRFs: BRPM.003 = BRNB.002, 004 = 003, 005 = 004, 011 = 007, 012 = 008, 013 = 009, 014 = 010, 018 = 011, 019 = 012, 020 = 013, 021 = 014, 022 = 015 and 024 = 016. The platform capability for these rows is already built for BRD-1, mainly in the `catalog`, `nonpackage`, `workflow`, `messaging`, `docgen`, `nbreport` and `audit` modules. The fit/gap below therefore concentrates on what is specific to packages.

### Big-ticket items

1. **Product versions and rate schemes (BRPM.007, PMADD06, BRPM.006).** Catalog records are changed in place today: `AuthorizableEntity.markModified()` puts an edited product back to PENDING_AUTHORIZATION, and while it waits `ProductCatalogService.requireUsableProduct` refuses it. Neither a quotation nor an account records which rate scheme priced it. BRD-3 needs versions with an effective date, a validation step before release, "latest approved scheme only" for new business, prior versions kept for renewals, a scheme stamp on every transaction, and an approval for any non-current rate.
2. **Package request lifecycle (BRPM.008-017).** A new workflow from the Package Request Form through two approval levels, TSU negotiation, ManCom sign-off and MBS set-up to release and advisory, followed by expiry monitoring and renewal. The negotiation parts follow the `nonpackage` implementation of BRNB.007-010 but produce a catalog product version instead of accounts.
3. **Insurer-specific package terms (PMADD01, PMADD02).** Coverage / peril level, insurer x coverage mapping (included, limit, deductible, warranties and clauses), and a hierarchy that cannot be saved incomplete.
4. **Incentive criteria on the products matrix (PMADD07, PMADD08).** The booking incentive rules (`bkg_incentive_rule`, V870) are not maker-checker and take free-text product codes. They move into a validated, effective-dated catalog master.

## 9. Observations on the BRD pack

- **Three documents in one PDF.** Pages 36-61 are an earlier signed version of the main BRD (up to 16-Nov-2025, BRQID numbering). The final version (pp.10-35) renumbers the IDs to BRPM. The main BRD approval pages say "for the OPERATIONS request" (p.34, p.59). The addendum revision log says "Added BRID PMADD07 and PMADD07", which should read PMADD07 and PMADD08 (p.2).
