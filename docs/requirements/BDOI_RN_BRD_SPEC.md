## 2. Business summary

**What the renewal process is.** BDOI renews the policies it has placed. Months before expiry, the list of expiring accounts is extracted. The legacy name of this list is "RMEL", which the BRD does not expand. Marketing then decides what to do with each account. This decision is the **disposition**:
- For Renewal;
- Not for Renewal, with a reason;
- For Quotation;
- For Proposal;
- Lost Business.

Processing prepares the renewal terms, sends the "For Renewal" accounts to each insurer for approval and receives the insurer's decision. It then issues a **Renewal Advice (RA)** to the client, as a first or second notice. After the client accepts, the renewal is placed and booked.

Letters cover the other outcomes:
- **NFR**, the Not for Renewal Letter, for non-renewable risk codes;
- **NAL**, the No Advice Letter, when neither an RA nor an NFR applies;
- reminder and non-acceptance letters for **NRNS** (No Renew / No Submit) accounts.

**Current process (p.42).**
- IT extracts the expiring list (RMEL).
- Dispositions are made in spreadsheets, and communication is by e-mail.
- Upload to QPS and the automatic RMEL text file for RAs work for packaged accounts only. The Business Units handle non-packaged accounts and exceptions by hand.
- Renewal status is monitored manually.

**Envisioned process (p.43, refined by both addenda).**
- There is **one process** for packaged and non-packaged accounts.
- The BU extracts the expiring list itself for any date range. The workshop adds an **automatic** extraction at configurable lead days, for example -140 days (BRRN.030). Extraction never starts a renewal by itself (BRRN.021).
- The system **sanitises, validates, matches and classifies** each record into Clean / Review / Exception buckets (BRRN.020/022/023). It applies a configurable **decision matrix**, so that clean renewals go straight through and exceptions are isolated (BRRN.031/034).
- **Online disposition** follows a staged workflow:
  1. the Marketing TL assigns the account to an AO (non-CBG only);
  2. the AO dispositions it;
  3. the TL reviews, returns or posts it;
  4. the Processing TL assigns it to a Processing Officer;
  5. the Processing Officer updates it and reviews the computations;
  6. Processing extracts it per insurer and sends it;
  7. the insurer's file is received;
  8. the RA is generated and sent in batch, encrypted.
- **Endorsements** must be reflected on the mother (expiring) policy before approval (BRRN.032). An account with **financial changes** takes the New Business or proposal path (BRRN.033/038).
- **Insurer responses** drive the status automatically: Renew As Is, Reject or Revise (BRRN.035).
- **Client acceptance** of the RA moves the renewal to placement and booking by itself (BRRN.040).
- **LAMD** (the bank loan unit; the acronym is not expanded in the BRD) provides the paid-off and RMU loan reports. They are matched by PN number and exclude or route the accounts (BRRN.024/029/039).
- **Contact Center** users follow up renewals in the system (BRRN.026).
- **Reports**: renewal status per invoice, a 34-line summary, centralised listings by unit and segment (UH, IBG, Leasing), dashboards per AO / PO, and export to .xlsx / .pdf with print preview.

**Personas.**
- Main BRD: Marketing Team Leader, Marketing Account Officer / Admin (with Account Broker in the usage table), Processing Team Leader, Processing Officer (and Broker), Business Administrator, System Administrator.
- Workshop addendum: Renewal Processor / User, **LAMD**, **Contact Center**, authorised user.
- Segments named: CBG, CLG (bulk), Non-Mortgage (individual), UH, IBG, Leasing and BBG. Bank and non-bank clients are distinguished for KYC.

**How it lands on BrokerVerse.**
- A renewal is a new **account** of business type RENEWAL, linked to the expiring account and invoice. Its ARN follows the NB convention, and the booking module already carries the business type NEW_BUSINESS / RENEWAL (BRNB.097/107).
- The candidate, disposition, insurer and letter lifecycle before that account exists is new. It is proposed as the `renewal` module.
- Renewal reuses the NB flow from the account onwards: quotation or PRF for the NB path, account, placement gate and slip, issuance and booking.
- It also reuses the Product Maintenance **RENEWAL rating purpose**, which already exists in `be/catalog/service/RatingQuery.java` and `SchemeResolver.java` and is still unused.

### Big-ticket items

1. **Renewal candidate lifecycle and workflow** (`renewal`, NEW, L): extraction, sanitation, matching, buckets, disposition, TL review, processing, insurer, RA and closure, as one work case per expiring invoice. The prototype tabs (Unassigned Disposition | For Renewal | For Quotation | Not for Renewal) and the Clean / Review / Exception pill come from BDO_UX_GUIDELINES §6 UX-2.
2. **Rules engine: sanitation checks, bucket rules and decision matrix** (BRRN.020/023/031/034, NEW, M+M+L). The rules are versioned, maker-checker and explainable, and every outcome is logged.
3. **Insurer round-trip** (3.009 / 4.007, BRRN.035, NEW, L): an encrypted extract per insurer, then an upload of the insurer's decision file with matching, automatic status progression and the negative scenarios.
4. **Letters and notices** (BRRN.001/009/010/025/037, NEW, M-L): the RA with first / second notice, generated and sent in batch with encryption; NAL, NFR, NRNS reminder and non-acceptance letters; the lock on RA generation.
5. **Acceptance to booking without re-keying** (BRRN.040, CHANGE, L): a renewal account is created from the expiring account and its product version. It is fast-tracked through the NB account workflow, then placed and queued for booking automatically, and it books with business type RENEWAL.
6. **Reports** (1.009 / 2.008 / 3.010 / 4.008, BRRN.019/036, NEW / CHANGE, M): the renewal status report with 40 filter criteria, 37-40 columns and a 34-line summary; centralised listings with escalation; dashboards per AO and PO.
7. **Account history view** (BRRN.027, CHANGE, M): policies, renewals, endorsements, payments and claims from the mother policy. Claims wait for the Claims BRD.

## 11. Observations on the BRD pack

- **Three documents, two addenda with overlapping IDs.** BRRN.001-019 (Addendum 1, Dec 2025) and BRRN.020-040 (Workshop addendum, Apr 2026) continue one numbering. Only Addendum 1 fills the "Source BRD ID" column. BRRN.020-040 are new requirements with no link to the main BRD, so their relation to the persona steps is inferred (section 4).
- **Online vs offline disposition.** The main BRD is "Online Dispositioning" (2.004), but BRRN.018 says "dispositioning is performed outside the system, and the system's role is to accept a batch upload". Both are designed; see RQ14.
- **Automation vs explicit control.** BRRN.021 forbids automatic progression after extraction. BRRN.031 / 039 / 040 require straight-through processing after initiation. The design reconciles them with an explicit initiation step, then STP (RQ02).
- **NRNS checkpoint.** BRRN.025 says both "+90 days" and "90 days before expiry" (RQ18).
- **Duplicate and mislabelled numbering in the main BRD.**
  - 1.003.4.1.1.21-24 are all labelled "1.21" (p.50).
  - 3.003.4.1.8-21 restart their labels at 1.2 (p.112-114).
  - The Processing Officer section (4.xxx) says "Log in with a **Operations Team Leader** user profile" (4.001.1.1, p.148).
  - The RA IDs of section D use "4.09.x" instead of "4.009.x", and 4.09.2.3 / 4.09.3 are shifted by one line (p.177-178).
  - The RA encryption item (x.010.7.1) is missing for the Processing Officer (4.09.7), although BRRN.010 applies to all.
  - Section "XI. Generate Renewal Advice" of the Processing Officer follows "VIII. Generate Report" (IX and X are missing).
- **Wording slips.**
  - The result of 2.004.9 reads "User should be able to provide disposition and edit locked accounts", the inverse of the requirement.
  - 3.005.1 says "Processing Office (PO)".
  - The title of 3.008.2.5 says "Return to the assigned Marketing TL" but its result says "Marketing AO or Marketing TL".
  - 1.006 is titled "Transfer to account to other units".
  - The addendum-1 source list of BRRN.002 includes "BRD 3.003.4.1.1" (an EBIX column, which is itself out of scope); it is probably 3.003.1.1.
- **Working notes left in signed pages:** "Discussed - 11/20/2025" (p.58), "Start here - next meeting 11/26/2025" (p.76), "Type text here" (p.78), "Resume here" (p.84). The page header of Addendum 1 reads "Renewal Addendum" while its approval text says "RMEL Phase 2 - Online Dispositioning request".
- **Undefined acronyms.** RMEL, LAMD, RMU (expanded once as Remedial Management Unit, 2.004.4.1), NRNS (expanded in BRRN.037), NAL, NFR / NRL, ARF, TSU, CBG, CLG, IBG, BBG, UH and PN have no acronym table.
- **Process Step column.** The main BRD rows carry a "Process Step" (1-6, "1 to 6" for reports), but the process flow they refer to is not in the pack.
- **Missing attachment.** The Combank / Corbank UH approval of the main BRD is "with email approval (see attached)", but the e-mail is not in the pack. Comptrollership's line on the main BRD is blank, and on Addendum 1 it says "not required for this BRD".
- **Priority.** Every row is "Must have". 5.005.1 has no priority.
- **NFRs refer to QPS**, which Addendum 1 declares out of scope (RQ28).
- **Summary counters** use statuses that no disposition, reason or step produces: For ARF, Renew to TSU, Disapproved, to Other Bank (RQ10).
- **"Approved domains"** for RA e-mails to clients is unclear (RQ16).
- **Overlap with BRD-1:** "Non-renewable Accounts" is both a reason the AO can pick (2.004.4.9) and a system tag (BRRN.009, 3.004.4). KYC visibility (BRRN.028) overlaps BRNB.110, which is already built.
- **Overlap with BRD-3:** BRPM.017 package renewal is the renewal of the **package** (the insurer agreement), not of client policies. The two are kept apart (PRODUCT_MAINTENANCE_DESIGN risk 5).
